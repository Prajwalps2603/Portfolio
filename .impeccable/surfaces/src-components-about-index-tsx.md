## Direction contract

THESIS: Credentials are valuables, so they are kept in a vault. A round bank-vault door with a combination dial and a spoked wheel guards a strongroom; beside it, a wall of safe-deposit boxes holds one credential each. It refuses the category default (a grid of logo badges or coloured cards) and makes opening it the visitor's own act.

OWN-WORLD: The ink slab stays; on it one strongroom wall (an ink-2 panel). The door is the site's lime (rim with rivets, lime-soft face), with paper-2 bolts, an ink wheel with lime grips, a paper dial with a tangerine marker and a "PMP" maker's plate; the strongroom behind the doorway is drawn in one-point perspective in ash lines with a hanging lamp. The deposit boxes: small numbered ones in rows (101-124) and four big ones with paper-2 faces carrying the facts (Inter title, mono issuer · year, blue Verify), each with a dark compartment and a little fog-steel door; inside each, the credential as an object (a framed certificate with a blue seal, a hexagonal lime badge, a rolled diploma with a tangerine ribbon, a lime trophy).

STORY: The visitor reaches the slab and finds the vault locked: door shut, bolts out, every box shut, the lamps dim; the wheel gives a small turn and back as a hint and a lime note under the door says "turn the wheel to open the vault". A click (a tap, Enter; or the wheel turned by hand past 200 degrees with a mouse) spins the dial left, right, left, turns the wheel, pulls the bolts back and swings the door open on its hinge; the strongroom's lamp comes on, and the deposit boxes open one by one, a lamp lighting for each. A second click locks it all again.

FIRST VIEWPORT (of the block, 1440 wide): "Certifications & awards" h3 top-left on the ink slab; under it the strongroom wall: the round door (about 25rem) on the left with the note under it, and on the right the header "Strongroom · deposit boxes" with four lamps, a row of ten small boxes, the four big boxes in a 2 x 2 grid, another row of small boxes. Below 1200px the door sits above the boxes; below 900px the boxes are one column.
